You describe it and it's yours in minutes

About as long as the kettle takes. You type the sentence, it builds while you find a mug, and by the time you sit down there is something to open.

How it works

Three small steps, no hard parts

1Say it

Write the idea the way you would say it to a friend. A countdown to the wedding. A wheel that picks who does the bins.

2Watch it take shape

Buttons, colours and the little bits of logic appear while you watch. Change your mind out loud and it changes with you.

3Keep it forever

It lives on your phone like any other app. Use it once for the party or every morning for a year, it is yours either way.

Got questions

Things people want to know

If yours is not here, write to us at hello@playful.app and a real person will answer.

Do I need to know how to code

No, and you never will. If you can send a text message you can make something with Playful. The sentence you type is the whole skill.

What kinds of apps can it make

Small, useful, personal things. Countdowns, trackers, wheels, quizzes, packing lists, chore rotas, a scoreboard for the games night that always ends in an argument. If it fits on one screen, it fits here.

How long does minutes actually mean

Usually two or three. Something with a lot of moving parts might take ten. You will see it building the whole time, so you are never sat wondering.

What does it cost

Nothing during the beta. Later there will be a simple price that we will tell you about well before it starts. No trial that quietly becomes a subscription, no surprises on your card.

Can I share what I make

Yes, every app has a link you can send to anyone. They open it and it just works, no account and no download. Keep it private if you would rather, that is one tap.
