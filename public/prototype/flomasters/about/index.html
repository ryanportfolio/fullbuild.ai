Meet the plumber

Every job has my name on it now

I'm Antonio Spence. I spent years running service calls under other people's names before I started Flomasters in 2024. This page is who you're actually hiring.

Why I went out on my own

Working for the big shops teaches you two trades. One is plumbing. The other is selling, and I never liked the second one. When your paycheck has a commission in it, every water heater looks like it needs replacing.

Flomasters is the shop I wished I'd worked for. There's no dispatcher, no sales targets, and no rotating cast of techs. When you call, you get the person doing the work. When I quote, the number comes from the job, not from a quota. And when something I fixed acts up, you call the man who fixed it, because there's only one.

The name is a promise about focus. Drains are the calls I grew up on: the Sunday backups, the roots in the line, the kitchen sink that quits during the holidays. That's the work I built this company around, and the rest of residential plumbing came with it.

The door moment

Letting a stranger into your house is the part nobody talks about. So here's exactly how it goes: I call or text before I head over, so you know when to expect me and who's coming. I show up in work clothes, I put on boot covers at the door, and I'll walk you through what I'm doing at whatever level of detail you want, including none.

If you'd rather compare notes first, my license number is at the bottom of every page on this site, and the state's lookup will show you it's real, current, and clean.

What I don't do

  • New construction rough-in
  • Whole-house remodels
  • Septic pumping
  • Commercial and multi-family buildings

I fix what's already in your house. If your job isn't on my list, I'll tell you on the phone, and I'll give you the name of someone good instead of a bad fit of a quote.

What a master license takes

Virginia doesn't hand this title out. Before you can sit for the master exam you need years as a licensed journeyman, or close to a decade of documented experience in the trade. The state's definition of the difference: a journeyman works to someone else's plan. A master plans the work.
