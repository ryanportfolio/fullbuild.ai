Local knowledge

Hampton Roads houses have Hampton Roads problems

This region sits low, wet and old. The plumbing under our houses fails in patterns you can learn, and after enough crawl spaces, you do.

What I see in Norfolk houses
  • Pre-war cast iron and galvanized in Norfolk and Portsmouth, narrowed by eighty years of rust until a Sunday dinner finishes it off
  • Gray polybutylene supply pipe in 1980s and 90s Chesapeake and Virginia Beach subdivisions, failing quietly from the inside
  • Crawl spaces over a high water table, where brackish ground moisture eats buried pipe from the outside in
  • Tree roots in clay sewer laterals under every old street with a good canopy, from Ghent to Riverview

More about me and how I work

What "Master Plumber" means in Virginia

It's not a word you get to pick for yourself. Virginia requires years in the trade as a licensed journeyman, or close to a decade of practical experience, before you can even sit for the master exam. A master plumber is qualified to plan and lay out the work, not just follow someone else's plan.

Questions I get on the phone

Fair questions, straight answers

Do you actually answer your own phone

Yes. If I'm under a sink with wet hands, it goes to voicemail, and texting the same number works better: I read texts between turns of a wrench. Either way I get back to you fast, usually within the hour during working hours.

What does it cost for you to come out

$79, and it comes off the bill when I do the work. That covers me showing up, diagnosing the problem, and giving you a flat written price. If you text me a photo first, I can often give you a realistic range before I drive over.

Are you licensed

I hold a Virginia Master Plumber tradesman license, #2710081569, issued by the Board for Contractors through DPOR. You don't have to take my word for it: the state runs a public license lookup, and I'd genuinely rather you check.

Do you work nights and weekends

My regular hours are Monday to Saturday, 7am to 7pm. After that, text me. If water is actively going where it shouldn't, I'll talk you through shutting it off tonight and be there first thing. I don't advertise 24/7 because I'm one person, and I'd rather be honest than sorry.

What parts of Hampton Roads do you cover

I'm based in Chesapeake. Chesapeake, Norfolk, Virginia Beach and Portsmouth are my everyday range. Suffolk, Hampton and Newport News I cover too, just with a little more honesty about drive time when you book.

Is there anything you don't do

New construction rough-in, whole-house remodels, septic pumping and commercial buildings. I fix what's already in your house. If your job isn't my kind of job, I'll tell you on the phone and point you to someone good.
