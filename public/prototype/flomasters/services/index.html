Services

Everything I do, and the honest range for each

Repair and service work in occupied houses. That's the specialty. Every job below gets a firm written price before I start, and the visit fee comes off the bill.

  1. Drain cleaning

    Kitchen sinks, tubs, showers, toilets and laundry lines. Cabled from the right access point and tested with a full-bore flush before I pack up.

     $149 to $325

  2. Main sewer line clearing

    Everything backing up at once means the main. Cleared from the cleanout, roots cut, and the camera run after so you can see it's actually open.

     From $249

  3. Sewer camera inspection

    Buying an older house, or tired of yearly backups? Watch your own line with me and get the footage. Free with any main line clearing.

     $189

  4. Hydro jetting

    For grease-packed kitchen lines and root-prone laterals that a cable only pokes holes through. High-pressure water scours the pipe wall clean.

     $395 to $650

  5. Water heater repair

    No hot water, rumbling tanks, pilot trouble, leaking valves. Most repairs happen the same visit with parts off the truck.

     From $129

  6. Water heater replacement

    When the tank itself goes, I'll say so and show you why. 40 and 50 gallon swaps installed, hauled away, done by dinner.

     $1,450 to $2,400

  7. Leak location and repair

    Pinholes in copper, weeping joints, the stain in the ceiling that keeps growing. Found first, then fixed, not guessed at.

     From $189

  8. Toilets, faucets and disposals

    Running toilets, dripping faucets, dead disposals. Repaired when it's worth it, swapped when it's not, and I'll tell you which.

     From $119

  9. Repipes

    Gray polybutylene and rusted galvanized don't heal. I replace failing runs before they flood, room by room or whole house, with a written quote first.

     Written quote

  10. Crawl space pipe repair

    Sagging drains, corroded supply lines, insulation hiding a slow leak. I do my best work where nobody wants to look.

     From $189

  11. Shutoff valves and hose bibbs

    The valve that won't turn is the one you'll need in an emergency. Replaced before it matters, including frost-proof outdoor spigots.

     From $99

  12. Something else

    If it's residential plumbing repair, ask. If it's not my kind of job, I'll say so on the phone and point you to someone good.

     Just call

Not doing: new construction rough-in, whole-house remodels, septic pumping, commercial buildings. One man does repair work best, so that's what this company sells.
