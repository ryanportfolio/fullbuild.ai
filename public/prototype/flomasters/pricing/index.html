Why a range and not one number

A kitchen clog twenty feet in is not a kitchen clog sixty feet in. What moves the price: how far the blockage is, whether there's a usable cleanout, how hard the part is to reach, and the fixture itself. What never moves it: who answers the phone, a commission, or how the day is going.

When I quote your job in writing, the range collapses to one number. That's the one you pay.

The warranty, in one sentence

My labor is covered for one full year, parts carry their manufacturer's warranty, and if my work fails inside that year I come back and make it right for free.
