Foxtail Drive-In · Paid media

The summer season

Subject
An invented drive-in cinema on Route 9 outside Hudson, New York, selling its summer season across display and paid social
System
Cream canvas, one electric blue, a compressed poster voice for announcements, a didone for the program, one illustrated car with a foxtail on the antenna
Constraint
The $45 season pass ends on opening night, June 12, so every unit, whatever its size, ladders to that date

The labels and the dates
