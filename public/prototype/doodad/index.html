Got questions

Things people want to know

If yours is not here, write to us at hello@doodad.example and a real person will answer.

Do I need to know how to code

No, and you never will. If you can send a text message you can make something with Doodad. The sentence you type is the whole skill.

What kinds of apps can it make

Small, useful, personal things. Countdowns, trackers, wheels, quizzes, packing lists, chore rotas, a scoreboard for the games night that always ends in an argument. If it fits on one screen, it fits here.

How long does minutes actually mean

Usually two or three. Something with a lot of moving parts might take ten. You will see it building the whole time, so you are never sat wondering.

What does it cost

Nothing during the beta. Later there will be a simple price that we will tell you about well before it starts. No trial that quietly becomes a subscription, no surprises on your card.

Can I share what I make

Yes, every app has a link you can send to anyone. They open it and it just works, no account and no download. Keep it private if you would rather, that is one tap.
