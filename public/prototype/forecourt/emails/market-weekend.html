October 3 and 4 · A market in the yard at Foundry Works
FORECOURT Hudson NY
One yard,
forty makers

On the first weekend of October the yard at Foundry Works becomes a market. The forty studios open their doors and set up tables between the halls, and three neighbors come along: the bookshop, the paper studio, and the drive-in.
                 

Dates  Saturday October 3 and Sunday October 4

Hours  Ten to five, both days, free to walk in

Where  The yard at Foundry Works, 12 Water Street, Hudson

Letterpress cards and a short run of market prints, pulled for this weekend only.
