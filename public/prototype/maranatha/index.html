Our Story

A regenerative forest farm in the Somerset Hills of New Jersey, healing the land one season at a time.

Where It All Began

Healthy food
that is delicious

Healthy soil makes healthy food. We are what we eat ate.

“Eating is an agricultural act.”Wendell Berry

In 2015

Michele and her family moved to the Somerset Hills of New Jersey

She felt a calling to combine her love for food with healing the land that was allowed to go fallow after being farmed more than ninety years ago. The fallow years left behind an unsuccessful successionary forest, invasive plants, and eroding soil.

Interdependence

Earth · animals · people

We honor the interdependence of the earth, animals, and people.

Heal the land

Our goal is to get back to basics.

High standard of care

We believe in a high standard of care for the animals who feed us, pastured in rotation as multi-species flocks.

Inspire others

Farmers are compensated fairly. It is our intention to inspire others to do what they can.
