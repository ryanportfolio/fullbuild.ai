Foredge Books · Email program proof

Four mailings from one system

Foredge Books is a fictional independent bookshop and small press, invented for this piece. Below are four of its marketing emails: a promotion, a welcome, a newsletter, and a seasonal campaign. Each one is a real responsive HTML file, rendered here live at desktop and mobile width. Open any of them and read the source.

01

The brief

Subject
A bookseller that publishes its own short list and mails the people who read it. The email program covers promotion, lifecycle, editorial, and seasonal work.
System
Editorial monochrome on warm paper. One warm near-black ink, one mid gray, a featherweight display serif, a tracked grotesque, pill controls. Hierarchy comes from scale and whitespace, never from color.
Constraint
Every email survives Outlook, reads at arm's length, and keeps every string as live text. Nothing on screen renders below 16 pixels, including the footer.

04

The engineering

  • LayoutNested tables with role="presentation" throughout, a hybrid 600 pixel container with an mso conditional wrapper, no flexbox or grid in the structure.
  • OutlookEvery pill button is a VML roundrect with the real anchor in the non-mso branch, and line heights carry mso-line-height-rule where they matter.
  • FontsSelf-hosted Fraunces and Inter behind an mso guard, with the full Georgia and Helvetica fallback stacks inline on every declaration, so Outlook falls straight to its fallback and never touches a webfont.
  • Preview textEach mailing carries a hidden preheader div that sets the inbox preview line.
  • Type floorNothing below 16 pixels anywhere, including the footer and legal line. Body runs 18, headlines run 48 to 56 in Fraunces 300.
  • ContrastInk on paper measures 17.44:1 and ash on paper 6.40:1 by the WCAG 2.1 relative luminance formula, so both pass AA at every size used.
  • Live textHeadlines, prices, buttons, and the book covers themselves are HTML text. With images blocked, every mailing still reads in full, and the photographs carry written alt text.
  • Accessibilitylang on the root, one h1 per mailing with a logical heading order below it, descriptive link text, and buttons padded past a 44 pixel touch target.

Colophon

Foredge Books is spec work: the shop, the press, the titles, and the authors are invented for this portfolio piece, and no affiliation with any retailer is implied. The mailings are table-based HTML written for this piece and rendered above from the shipped files.

Photography was generated with an image model and art-directed to the brand's warm register: blank cloth covers, kraft paper, no legible type. Typefaces are Fraunces and Inter, self-hosted. Concept 01 in the marketing design set.
