The garden suites

01

The brief

SUBJECT
An invented studio putting six semi-custom invitation suites on Instagram in one week. Hollyhock, Bryony, Yarrow, Campion, Tansy, Mallow, all named for what grows in a June garden.
SYSTEM
Blush canvas, ink navy type, coral held back for pills and the last call. Cormorant Garamond at poster scale carries every headline; Inter carries the labels, the dates, and the fine print.
CONSTRAINT
Couples marrying in June have to order by April 3, so the whole week funnels to one date: tease it, launch it, explain it, then call it.
