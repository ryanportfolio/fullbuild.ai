FOXGLOVE PAPER · BRAND SYSTEM

How it is drawn

01

The signature

LOCKUP

A line of Inter caps, then a rule. All ten tiles open this way, so the shape reads as Foxglove before a word is read. The eyebrow sets in Inter 500 at 0.2em, the rule sits 32px under it and measures 88 by 4.

CLEAR SPACE

One rule width, 88px, from any edge of the canvas. Story tiles push the top inset to 200px so the platform chrome cannot land on it.

GROUND

The rule is coral on blush. When the whole frame turns coral, the rule turns ink instead, because coral on coral is a rule nobody can see.

THE NAME

The studio name is always Inter caps in the eyebrow slot. Cormorant carries what the post says, never who is saying it.

The type

LABELS THE INVITATION · THE RSVP CARD · THE DETAILS
BODY Cormorant Garamond holds weight 300 at poster scale. Inter carries the labels and the dates.
TWO FACES

Cormorant Garamond for display and headings, italic for couple names. Inter for labels, dates, body and every control. Nothing else is added.

ONE WEIGHT

Cormorant is drawn at 300 only. The face was picked for the thin cut; at 500 it fills in and stops looking engraved.

TRACKING

Labels 0.2em, body 0.03em, display -0.01em on the tiles. The label tracking is the loudest tell in the system, so it never varies.

EXPORT SCALE

On the 1080px board: eyebrow 30px, support copy 32px to 46px, display 144px to 250px. The display size is set by how few words the tile carries, not by how important it is.

FLOOR

Nothing renders below 16px, on a tile or on the web.

05

The canvas

FEED

1080 by 1350. Type sits inside 88px sides and 96px top and foot.

STORY

1080 by 1920. Copy starts at 200px from the top inside 96px sides, and nothing that must read falls below 1364px, which is where the last call pill ends. The bottom of a story belongs to the platform and to the illustration.

WEB

One 1200px sheet, centered, 40px gutters. Under 1024px every post stacks and the tile takes the width of the column.

06

The illustration

FLAT

Flat shapes over line work. No gradient, no shadow, no texture, on any surface in the system.

BLEED

A drawing enters from an edge and leaves the frame. A shape that stops short inside the canvas reads as a sticker, and the campaign has none.

PLACEMENT

One corner per tile, opposite the copy. Two corners only when the canvas is wide enough to keep them apart.

TYPE OVER ART

Type never sits on the drawing. The copy holds its own field of blush, which is why the corners are corners.

07

The bans

  • No colour outside the palette, and no black. Ink navy is the darkest value in the system.
  • No gradients and no shadows, on a tile, on a pill, or on a card.
  • No em dashes, en dashes or curly quotes, anywhere the reader can see them.
  • No period at the end of a heading or a display line. The line ends because it is finished.
  • No type below 16px.
  • No pixel dimensions, file names or export notes on a tile. The frame is the only thing a reader sees.
